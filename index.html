BEFORE YOU ASK

A few little details.

Can I request a custom cake?+

Yes. Share your occasion, date and idea using the cake enquiry form. Our team will confirm the design, flavour, size and price. You can share a reference photo directly when speaking with us.

How much will my cake cost?+

Custom cake pricing depends on the size, design and details. Send an enquiry for your exact quote. Any prices shown in our range apply to the unit stated and are confirmed by the shop before you order.

Can I arrange delivery?+

Contact the shop to confirm delivery availability and charges for your area.

Can I ask about ingredients or allergies?+

Please speak with our team before ordering and tell us about any allergies or dietary requirements so we can check the ingredients and preparation.
